# Jireh Natural Foods — Register Manual

**For everyone who serves customers.** You do not need to read this end to end.
Section 2 gets you through your first shift. Everything after it is there for
the day you need it.

Kept next to the code so it stays true. Last updated 3 August 2026.

---

## 1. The one-minute version

| You want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Start your day | Open the shift, enter the cash already in the drawer |
| Sell something | Tap the food → tap **Charge** → pick how they paid |
| Customer eats first, pays later | Tap **Send to kitchen · pay later** |
| Take money for a ticket | Tap the yellow ticket at the top → pick how they paid |
| Something ran out | Press and hold the food, choose **Take off menu** |
| End your day | **Shift** → count the drawer → **Close Shift** |

**The golden rule:** if the screen and the money in your hand disagree, trust
the money and tell your manager. Never change a number to make it match.

## 2. Your first shift, step by step

### 2.1 Sign in

Go to the register page and sign in with your own email and password.

Use **your own** login, never a colleague's. Every order records who rang it
up. If you use someone else's account, their name goes on your mistakes — and
on your good work.

Forgot your password? Ask the owner. Nobody, including the person who built
this, can read your password — it can only be replaced.

### 2.2 Open the shift

A **shift** is one working period at the till. It is how the business knows
what money should be in the drawer at the end of the day.

1. Count the cash already in the drawer before you sell anything.
2. Type that number as the **Opening Float**.
3. Tap **Open Shift & Start Selling**.

> If the drawer starts empty, enter **0**. Do not guess or round. This number
> is what everything at closing time is measured against.

**You cannot take orders until a shift is open.** That is deliberate — an order
with no shift is money nobody can account for.

#### If it says "Stale Shift Needs Review"

Somebody left yesterday's shift open. You have three choices:

- **Continue on Existing Shift** — you are carrying on the same working period.
- **Close Stale Shift** — count what is in the drawer and close it properly.
- **Close Stale & Open New Shift** — close yesterday's and start fresh. This is
  usually what you want in the morning.

Anyone can do this. You do not need a manager.

### 2.3 Take an order

1. **Tap the food.** Tap it twice for two portions, or use **+** in the order
   panel.
2. **Answer the questions if any appear.** Some dishes ask how the customer
   wants it — grilled or fried, mild or hot, any extras. Required questions are
   marked; the **Add** button stays greyed out until you answer them.
3. **Check the order panel** on the right (tap **Cart** at the bottom on a
   phone). Extras appear in green under the item name.
4. **Choose Dine In, Takeaway or Delivery.**
5. **Tap Charge.**

### 2.4 Take the money

Pick how the customer paid:

- **Cash** — type what they handed you. The screen shows the change to give
  back. Give exactly that.
- **MoMo** — type the amount received and the reference if you have it.
- **Bolt Food** — for orders that came through Bolt.
- **Split** — see §4.1.

Then tap the green button to finish. The receipt prints automatically.

> The amount on the **Charge** button is exactly what the system records. It
> can never be more than what you see.

## 3. Things that happen every day

### 3.1 Something ran out (the 86 board)

"86" is kitchen shorthand for *we are out of it*.

**Press and hold** the item on the menu grid → **Take off menu**.

That instantly:
- greys it out on the register so nobody sells it by mistake,
- hides it from the website so customers stop ordering it.

When it comes back, press and hold again → **Put back on menu**.

Do this the moment the kitchen tells you. One minute of delay is one more
customer being sold something you cannot serve.

### 3.2 Customer eats now, pays later

For dine-in, tap **Send to kitchen · pay later** instead of Charge.

The food is fired and the ticket appears as a **yellow chip at the top of the
menu**, showing the amount and how many minutes it has been waiting.

When they are ready to pay: **tap the yellow chip** → pick how they paid. Done.

> The screen says **"Sent to Kitchen"**, not "Order Complete" — because no money
> has been taken yet. Do not let the customer leave until that chip is gone.

### 3.3 Fixing an order before you charge

- **Wrong quantity** — use **−** and **+** on the order line.
- **Wrong item** — tap the bin icon on that line.
- **Wrong extras** — bin the line and add it again with the right choices.
- **Start over** — **Clear** at the top of the order panel.

The same dish ordered two different ways stays as **two separate lines**. One
line of "Jollof, grilled" and one of "Jollof, fried" is correct, not a bug.

### 3.4 Adding a note

Tap **Add note** on any line — "no pepper", "pack separately". The note prints
on the kitchen ticket.

## 4. Less common, still useful

### 4.1 Split payment

Customer paying part cash, part MoMo:

1. On the payment screen, turn on **Split**.
2. Enter each amount.
3. The parts must add up to the exact total — the button stays locked until
   they do. That is on purpose.

### 4.2 Discounts

Enter the discount amount on the payment screen. It comes off before any levy.
A discount can never be larger than the bill.

Follow whatever the owner has told you about who may authorise discounts. The
system records who applied it.

### 4.3 The internet went down

**Keep selling.** The register works offline.

- A banner tells you orders are being saved on the device.
- Every order is kept safely and syncs by itself when the connection returns.
- You will see "**X offline orders waiting to sync**". That is normal.

**Do not** close the browser, clear the browser data, or hand the device to
someone else while orders are still waiting. They live on that device until
they sync.

Once it is back, the counter drops to zero. If it does not after a few minutes,
tap **Sync now**, and tell your manager if it still will not clear.

### 4.4 Reprinting a receipt

**Orders** at the bottom → find the order → **Print**.

## 5. Closing your shift

Do this at the end of every working period, before you hand over or go home.

1. Tap **Shift** at the bottom.
2. **Count the drawer.** Use **Count notes** and tap **+** for each note and
   coin. The total adds itself up. This is more reliable than counting in your
   head and typing one number — and if the total is wrong, you can see which
   denomination you miscounted.
   (Prefer to type one figure? Switch to **Type total**.)
3. Enter MoMo and Bolt amounts received if the shift took any.
4. Check the **Difference**.
5. Tap **Close Shift**.

### Understanding the three numbers

| | Means |
|---|---|
| **Expected in drawer** | Opening float + all cash sales this shift |
| **Counted** | What you physically counted just now |
| **Difference** | Counted − Expected |

**"Exact"** means they match. Otherwise it shows how much over or short.

If there is a difference, the system asks you to confirm before closing. **Do
not adjust your count to make it match.** Count again. If it is still different,
close the shift with the true number and tell your manager. An honest
difference is a small problem; a false count is a serious one.

Common innocent reasons: change given from your own pocket, a cash sale rung up
as MoMo, or a payout taken from the drawer without a note.

## 6. When something looks wrong

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "No open shift" | You have not opened a shift | Open one (§2.2) |
| "Session is closed" | The shift was closed elsewhere | Open a new shift |
| Item is grey and will not add | It has been taken off the menu | Ask the kitchen; put it back if it is available |
| "One of the selected options is no longer available" | An extra was removed while you were ordering | Bin the line, add it again |
| "This ticket has already been paid" | Somebody settled it already | Check Orders before charging again |
| "Your session expired" | You were signed out | Sign in again — nothing is lost |
| Screen frozen or blank | Usually the connection | Reload the page. Your order is saved. |

**Nothing you do here can lose an order.** Orders are saved before payment is
taken, kept on the device when offline, and cannot be created twice by
double-tapping. If you are unsure whether something went through, check
**Orders** rather than ringing it up again.

## 7. What you are not able to do

Some things are deliberately kept away from the register:

- **Change prices.** Prices come from the menu manager. If a price is wrong,
  tell the owner — do not work around it with a discount.
- **Delete an order.** Orders can be voided by a manager, with a reason. This
  keeps the day's takings honest.
- **See profit or cost figures.** Those live in the back office.

None of this is about trust. It is so that when the numbers are questioned,
they can be defended.

---

## 8. Habits of a good shift

- Open the shift **before** the first customer, not after.
- 86 items **the moment** you hear about them.
- Settle open tickets **before** the customer stands up.
- Count the drawer **honestly**, every time.
- Never share a login.
- If the screen and reality disagree, **reality wins** — say something.
